# Bibliography

Citations are organised by tag (the short identifier used inline
throughout the dossier). Tags are mostly first-author + year.

> Source-type notation: **[journal]** = peer-reviewed primary literature;
> **[book]** = textbook/monograph; **[review]** = review article in a
> peer-reviewed journal; **[doc]** = vendor datasheet or software docs;
> **[preprint]** = arXiv/preprint, not peer-reviewed; **[gov]** = NIST or
> equivalent technical reference.
